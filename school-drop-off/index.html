Who this kit is for

This kit is for parents whose school drop-off is the hardest five minutes of the day — the clinging, the tears, the guilt of walking away.

It works especially well for children who find separation hard and need a predictable, consistent goodbye. Most children under 8 do better at drop-off when they know exactly what to expect.

Common questions

When should I use this kit?

Read through the drop-off plan together a night or two before the school year starts, then keep the First-Then-Next board handy for the harder mornings.

Will this stop the goodbye from being hard?

Not necessarily, and that's not the goal. It gives your child a predictable, consistent goodbye and a way to tell you what they need — which for many children makes the moment more manageable, not painless.
