Who this kit is for

This kit is for parents preparing a child for a vaccination visit, whether it's a routine shot or a first-time appointment somewhere new.

It works especially well for children who need to know what to expect, including that there might be a quick pinch. Honesty, delivered calmly and in advance, tends to build more trust than a surprise.

Common questions

Does the kit promise it won't hurt?

No — the kit prepares your child honestly for a quick pinch, rather than promising it won't hurt. Honesty builds more trust than a surprise.

When should I use this kit?

Preview the visit plan together 1–3 nights before the appointment, and keep the comfort plan and communication cards handy on the day.
