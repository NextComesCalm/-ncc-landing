Who this kit is for

This kit is for parents whose child is starting a new school year — or a new school — and needs to know what the day will actually look like.

It works especially well for children who find new environments and new faces overwhelming. Most children under 8 do better on the first day when they've had a chance to picture it in advance.

Common questions

When should I use this kit?

Read the visual plan together a few nights before school starts, then again the night before. Repeat it if your child finds the new school year particularly worrying.

Is this useful for a new school, not just a new school year?

Yes — the plan and comfort pages work for any first day, whether it's a new grade at a familiar school or a brand-new one.
