Common questions

When should I use this kit?

Walk through the morning plan together the evening before, or first thing in the morning before the routine starts. Keep the First-Then-Next board somewhere visible during the rush.

Do I need to print it?

No — the PDF works fine on a phone or tablet propped up in the kitchen. Print it if a physical chart on the wall works better for your family.
