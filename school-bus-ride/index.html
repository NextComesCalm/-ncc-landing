What's inside the kit

One 8-page PDF: cover, Quick Start, What Happens, First-Then-Next, What I Might See or Hear, My Comfort Plan, I Can Tell You, and If the Bus Ride Feels Hard.

What Happens

A visual plan for waiting at the stop, boarding, finding a seat, the ride, and getting off.

First, Then, Next

Makes the sequence of the ride concrete, from the stop to the school doors.

What I Might See or Hear

The noise, the other kids, the motion of the bus — named plainly, not as a list of worries.

My Comfort Plan

A seat preference, a comfort item, or a small routine that helps on the ride.

I Can Tell You

Communication cards for the moments words are hard to find on a moving bus.

If the Bus Ride Feels Hard

Pause, breathe, ask for help — a plan for when the ride becomes too much.

Who this kit is for

This kit is for parents whose child is nervous about the noise, the strangers, or simply not being in control on the school bus.

It works especially well for a child's first bus ride, or a route or bus change. Most children under 8 feel more confident once they know exactly what to expect.

Common questions

When should I use this kit?

Preview the ride a few nights before your child's first bus ride, then keep the PDF on your phone for a quick reminder before the bus arrives.

What if my child rides a different route or bus?

The plan covers the general steps of a bus ride — waiting, boarding, finding a seat, the ride, and getting off. Talk through anything specific to your child's route in your own words.
